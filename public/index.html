About the Show

Every episode, every angle.

Survivor High Tide is your weekly episode recap and analysis show. Each week Ben and Oscar break down who is playing the best, dissect the edit, dig into the twists, and hand out fantasy Survivor tips. Equal parts analytical and entertaining, it's Survivor coverage that feels like the group chat came to life.

Ben

Ben is a co-host of Survivor High Tide and a five-time Survivor applicant who lives and breathes the game. He brings sharp, witty analysis and a reputation for hot takes that are as bold as they are entertaining. He also runs the Survivor High Tide fantasy league, where the competition is just as cutthroat as the show itself.

Oscar

Oscar is a co-host of Survivor High Tide and the show's resident Survivor historian, with a gift for connecting current gameplay to the broader history of the show. Each week he hands out the coveted Burnsy Award to the Survivor who had the best week in the game. Oscar will go on record that Boston Rob is overrated and he has a well-documented crush on Dee Valladares, and frankly, nobody can blame him.
